# Family Vault

## One-liner

Secure cross-platform vault for family documents, insurance cards, and renewal alerts.

## What it does

Family Vault is a secure cross-platform vault for family documents: IDs, insurance policies, passports, vehicle registrations, and critical papers — with automated renewal and expiration alerts and emergency share links. It follows a zero-knowledge storage model: encryption keys are derived on your own device, so the cloud infrastructure hosting the vault never possesses the keys to decrypt your files.

## Who it's for

Families who need one safe place for important documents and don't want to chase renewal dates manually.

## Status

Currently in development — not publicly available yet. When it launches it will be listed at snaplabs.tech.

## FAQ

**Is my data readable by the service?**
No. Family Vault uses zero-knowledge storage — keys are derived on your device, so the hosted infrastructure cannot decrypt your files.

**Which platforms will it support?**
It is being built as a cross-platform app for mobile (iOS and Android) and web.

**Who built it?**
Family Vault is being developed by the SnapLabs team.
