# RERA Sentinel

## One-liner

Regulatory lake buffer compliance & GIS geometry verification for homebuyers.

## What it does

RERA Sentinel helps homebuyers in Hyderabad verify residential developments against official regulatory boundaries before investing. It checks a property against HMDA Lake Full Tank Level (FTL) boundaries, the statutory 30m lake buffer zone, 9m nala (stormwater stream) channels, and TSRERA compliance filings enforced under WALTA and HYDRAA.

## Who it's for

Homebuyers and investors evaluating plots or apartments near lakes and streams who want to avoid statutory buffer encroachments — a real, costly mistake that is easy to make without verified data.

## Status

Live — available at https://rera-sentinel.snaplabs.tech

## FAQ

**What problem does it solve?**
Buying property inside a statutory buffer zone can make the investment illegal or undevelopable. RERA Sentinel surfaces boundary proximity warnings up front so buyers don't invest in encroachments.

**Who built it?**
