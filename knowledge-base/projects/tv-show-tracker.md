# TV Show Tracker

## One-liner

Intelligent episode timeline tracker & personalized discovery progressive web app.

## What it does

TV Show Tracker is a mobile-first progressive web app for tracking television shows: per-episode watch tracking, upcoming broadcast schedules, personalized discovery recommendations based on your viewing habits, and offline support through a service worker.

## Who it's for

Anyone who watches series across platforms and loses track of episodes, air dates, and what to watch next.

## Status

Live — available at https://tv-tracker.snaplabs.tech

## FAQ

**Can I use it offline?**
Yes — it's a progressive web app with offline support, so your watch history stays available without a connection.

**How does it recommend shows?**
Recommendations are personalized from your viewing habits and watch history.

**Who built it?**
TV Show Tracker was developed by the SnapLabs team.
